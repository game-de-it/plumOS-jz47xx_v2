plumOS frontend artwork

Scope: default system icons and ROM gallery background carried over from plumOS
A30, including matching copies used by 350teric, PyMenu and EmulationStation.

Source revision: 68d92d5d950f4d2e247316cf0d7bac776e6d6861
https://github.com/game-de-it/plumOS-A30/tree/68d92d5d950f4d2e247316cf0d7bac776e6d6861/package/frontend/plumos/themes/default

during JZ47xx_v2 image preparation. The decoded pixels match the source.
