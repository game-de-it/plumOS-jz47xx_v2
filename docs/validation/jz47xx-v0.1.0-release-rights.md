All 34 shared objects (24 distinct payloads) from the OPKs match retained build outputs, directly or after the original MIPS strip step. The inventory records source, notice and dynamic dependencies per file. It covers SDL_image and libpng in six emulators, Flite and libzip in Bard, GMU plugins and audio libraries, and FFmpeg in FFPlay. The FFmpeg build uses shared libraries and no GPL, version3 or nonfree enable flag. The eventual release needs the corresponding source, applicable LGPL replacement and debugging rights, and the FFmpeg notice on the download page. [FFmpeg compliance guidance](https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html)

The exact fMSX source is the unmodified Libretro revision `4de11755`. The author's general public notice allows attributed non-commercial use. The bundled license asks authors of ports or modifications to make contact. A grant covering this planned distribution remains unconfirmed. The core did not contain the complete bytes of any of the 12 ROM files from that source tree. [Author's notice](https://www.emulation.org/) [Pinned fMSX license](https://github.com/libretro/fmsx-libretro/blob/4de11755ce4f196ac1c8a7bb20bb4eccbc87a7d4/LICENSE)

FBA44 uses the pinned FB Alpha license. It requires publication of modifications and the verbatim license and sets conditions on profit, donations, game copyright dates, ROMs and DLL use. The source scan found 12,085 driver declarations. The latest readable year is 2019, and 540 entries are ranges, annotations or unspecified years. The binary dynamically links SDL, SDL_image, libpng, zlib and ALSA among other dependencies. Application of the DLL term to those Linux dependencies remains open. The next staging build includes the verbatim license for FBA44 and the GPLv3 notice and text for PokeMini. The two executable digests stayed identical in the staging check. [FBA44 license](https://github.com/plrguez/fba-sdl/blob/9448a9c6f1040ad8ec204b5e9cb83e25574ac0c8/src/license.txt)

The artwork inventory covers 677 files. The converted boot logo matches the repository-owned artwork documented by the plumOS ports. The other 31 frontend images match the V90S, A30 and Bubble themes. Rights for third-party elements within those images still need file-level documentation. All 410 rootfs images match GMenu2X or Buildroot inputs. The OpenDingux theme carries a CC BY 4.0 notice and a source link, and other wallpapers have separate terms. Of 235 OPK images, 232 match source or retained package assets. The two PocketSNES images and the RACE icon lack a verified source. Package equality alone cannot establish the artwork's redistribution terms. [V90S notices](https://github.com/game-de-it/plumOS-V90S_V2/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) [A30 notices](https://github.com/game-de-it/plumOS-A30/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) [OpenDingux CC BY theme](https://github.com/OpenDingux/gmenu2x-theme)

## Follow-up on the current candidate

The current candidate is `plumOS-JZ47xx-0.1.0.img`, raw SHA-256 `97767f5fde51de275c148ae8b3b55fbdf7391ef2059202d1e88f9d643e0dae65`. Its executables match the earlier audited payloads. The candidate carries both languages of the notices and the verbatim FBA44 and PokeMini license texts.
